# The Suite Book: design brief (owner, 8 Nov 2026: "This one really needs to look the part. Elegant.")

The subject: the 100 most luxurious hotels in the world. The reader is choosing where to spend US$2,000 to US$20,000 a night, or dreaming about it. The page has to feel like the hotels do: quiet, confident, nothing shouting.

## Direction
- The photograph is the luxury. Full-bleed, large, unhurried; one image at a time far more than grids. No badges, no chips on photos, no hover tricks on the hero.
- Space as the signal of price: wide margins, long pauses between sections, few things per screen.
- One serif, set large and light, carrying the identity: Bodoni Moda (high contrast, didone, the vocabulary of fashion mastheads and old hotel stationery) for display; a quiet grotesk for small text (Inter Tight or Manrope, small sizes only). No heavy/light mixing, no italic flourishes beyond one per page.
- Palette: pure white paper, near-black ink #121212, one deep accent used sparingly, midnight #1c2733 (buttons, the footer); hairlines in #e4e2dd. No gold, no cream, no gradients.
- Small caps-free: sentence case labels, letterspacing only on the wordmark.
- Wordmark: "The Suite Book" in Bodoni Moda, generous tracking, centred on the home page, small and left in the header elsewhere.
- Motion: one slow fade on the home hero image; nothing else moves on its own.

## Pages
- Home: one full-screen photograph that changes on each visit (from the 100), the wordmark, one line ("The hundred most extraordinary hotels in the world"), then the hundred as a quiet index by region: name, place, a small photo on hover on desktop.
- Hotel page: full-bleed lead photo, name and place, a short answer-first paragraph, then long-form photographs alternating with prose, a restrained facts table (rooms, opened, operator, nightly price from, nearest airport), the official site and the booking link, sources.
- Region and theme pages ("The most luxurious hotels in Paris", "Private islands", "Safari lodges"): editorial, numbered only where the order is meaningful.
- Booking: "Check availability" through Stay22 where the hotel is listed; otherwise "Reserve on the hotel's own site" (no affiliate link).
